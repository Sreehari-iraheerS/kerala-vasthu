4. Mandatory Construction Rules on Site (പ്രധാന നിബന്ധനകൾ)

  • Well (കിണർ): Strictly in North-East (ഈശാനകോൺ). Never in Kanni Moola (SW) or Agni Kone (SE).
  • Septic Tank: Strictly in North-West (വായുകോൺ) or West. Maintain mandatory 7.5m (25 ft) from drinking well.
  • Kitchen: Gas stove counter in South-East (അഗ്നികോൺ). Cook faces East during preparation.
  • Master Bed Cot: Headboard towards South or East. Head towards North is strictly prohibited.
  • Center (Brahmasthanam): Keep central core free of load-bearing pillars, stairways, or waste lines.
